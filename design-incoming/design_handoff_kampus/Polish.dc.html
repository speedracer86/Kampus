Tap it to open per-grade Review rows. Grades above say what earns them. Edge cases: a true grade-3 student has nothing below, and a grade-8 student ends at Graduation. 18a is the recommendation; the fourth column is the per-grade alternative.

19 · Wishlist tiles that never look broken
Four tile states: loading (a soft shimmer while the preview service fetches), loaded (the product photo), missed (a store-tinted initial with a category glyph) and link-only (nothing came back, so the tile shows the domain). Tap "Replay the fetch" to watch rows load and resolve.

InitialThe store's first letter, 28/600 in ink.
GlyphA 22px white disc in the bottom right holding the category icon (toy, book, art, game, sport, other).
